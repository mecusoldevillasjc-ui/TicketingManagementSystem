How it works

Three steps, and you can see where things stand at every one of them.

01

Submit

Choose a category, say how urgent it is, and describe the problem. Sign in on the web portal, or walk up to the kiosk.

02

Get assigned

Urgent tickets go to the top of the queue. An agent takes it, and you can see who is handling your request.

03

Resolve and rate

Follow the progress, reply to the agent, then confirm it is solved and rate the help you received.

Questions

Do I need an account to report a problem?

Not at the kiosk: anyone can submit there. On the web portal you create a free client account, which lets you see all your tickets in one place.

How do I check on a ticket?

Use "Track a ticket" and enter your ticket number (for example TCK-0042). You will see its progress. Private details stay hidden unless you sign in.

Who can see my ticket?

You and the support team. Other clients can never see your tickets.

What do the priorities mean?

High means you cannot work, medium means it slows you down, and low means it can wait. Higher priorities have shorter service targets.
